# Code of conduct

DiskMap is a small project run by one person in their free time. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their experience, background or identity.

**Please:**
- Be kind and assume good intent. Most people asking questions are new to something.
- Keep criticism about the code and the app, never about the person.
- Accept "no" on a feature idea gracefully. Not every good idea fits this app.

**Not okay, anywhere in this project:**
- Insults, slurs, harassment, or personal attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Posting someone's private information.
- Spam, or repeatedly pushing a topic after a decision was made.

**What happens:** comments that break this are hidden or deleted, and their authors may be blocked from the project, without debate. To report something, open an issue or contact the maintainer on Discord (**iurijedi**).

This is based on the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
